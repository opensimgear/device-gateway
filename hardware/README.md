# Mechanical hardware

Keep editable CAD, drawings, mechanical BOMs, and assembly instructions here. Lite and Pro are editions within this repository, not branches or separate repositories.

- [Lite](variants/lite/README.md): intended for 3D-printed construction.
- [Pro](variants/pro/README.md): alternative construction; laser cutting is a candidate, not a finalized choice.

When a design exists, add independently identified revision folders under its edition (for example lite/rev-a). Keep shared mechanical parts once under a common folder if needed. Generated print/cut/manufacturing exports belong in device releases alongside compatibility information.

No CAD files, mechanical revisions, or manufacturing-ready designs are provided yet.
